# Statement of Work

**Between:** Acme Corp ("Acme") and Initech Partners LLP ("Initech")
**Reference:** C-1003  **Effective date:** 15 May 2027  **Term:** 6 months
**Total fees:** USD 80,000

## 1. Scope
Initech will deliver a data-migration and reporting project for Acme in three phases, each with
written acceptance criteria agreed by both parties.

## 2. Fees
Fees are invoiced monthly in arrears and payable within 45 days.

## 3. Liability
Each party's liability is capped at the total fees under this Statement of Work.

## 4. Confidentiality
Each party will keep the other's confidential information secret for two years after the end of
the Statement of Work.

## 5. Termination
Either party may terminate for material breach not remedied within 30 days of written notice.

## 6. Governing law
This Statement of Work is governed by the laws of the State of New York.
