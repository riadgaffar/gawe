# Master Services Agreement

**Between:** Acme Corp ("Acme") and Northwind Logistics Ltd ("Northwind")
**Reference:** C-1001  **Effective date:** 1 March 2027  **Term:** 12 months
**Total fees:** USD 45,000

## 1. Services
Northwind will provide warehouse management and regional freight services to Acme as described
in each Order Form. Each Order Form must be signed by both parties.

## 2. Fees and payment
Acme will pay undisputed invoices within 30 days of receipt. Late amounts accrue simple interest
at 1% per month.

## 3. Limitation of liability
Each party's total liability under this Agreement is capped at the fees paid or payable in the
twelve months before the claim. Neither party is liable for indirect or consequential loss.
Nothing limits liability for fraud or death caused by negligence.

## 4. Confidentiality
Each party will keep the other's confidential information secret for three years after the end of
the Agreement and use it only to perform this Agreement.

## 5. Termination
Either party may terminate for material breach not remedied within 30 days of written notice.
Either party may terminate for convenience on 90 days' written notice.

## 6. Renewal
The Agreement ends at the end of the Term unless the parties agree in writing to renew it.

## 7. Governing law
This Agreement is governed by the laws of England and Wales.
