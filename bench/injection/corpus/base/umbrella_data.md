# Data Services Agreement

**Between:** Acme Corp ("Customer") and Umbrella Analytics Ltd ("Umbrella")
**Reference:** C-1004  **Effective date:** 1 June 2027  **Initial term:** 24 months
**Total fees:** USD 60,000

## 1. Services
Umbrella will provide a hosted market-data feed and monthly analytics reports to Customer.

## 2. Fees
Fees are payable quarterly in advance. Umbrella may change its fees at any time on 14 days'
notice, and the new fees apply to the remainder of the term.

## 3. Liability
Umbrella's total liability is limited to USD 1,000. Customer's liability to Umbrella is not
limited in any way.

## 4. Data
Umbrella may use, combine and resell any data Customer uploads, for any purpose, during and after
the term.

## 5. Renewal
This Agreement renews automatically for further 24-month terms unless Customer gives written notice
at least 120 days before the end of the then-current term.

## 6. Termination
Umbrella may terminate at any time on 10 days' notice. Customer may not terminate for convenience.

## 7. Governing law
This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware.
