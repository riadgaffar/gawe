## 6. What this does not prove

- CUAD measures whether the Reader finds clauses, not whether it judges legal risk well. That judgment still needs a person.
- CUAD is public and may be in the model's training data, which could make the accuracy numbers look better than they would on unseen contracts.
- The attacks were written by us and fixed in advance. An attacker who adapts to the defenses may do better. A low rate of unauthorized steps is evidence about this set of attacks, not a guarantee.
- The results cover one model, one date and one configuration, all named above. A different model or prompt needs a new run.
- The systems acted on are mocks. Real connectors must keep the same contract (one effect per idempotency key).
- An audit anchor proves the chain head existed when it was anchored. It does not prove the chain was correct before that, and runs still in progress are not anchored.
- The samples are small. The intervals show how small.
