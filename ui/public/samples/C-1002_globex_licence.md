# Software Licence and Support Agreement

**Between:** Acme Corp ("Customer") and Globex Systems Inc ("Globex")
**Reference:** C-1002  **Effective date:** 1 April 2027  **Initial term:** 36 months
**Total fees:** USD 250,000

## 1. Licence
Globex grants Customer a non-exclusive licence to use the Software for Customer's internal
business. Globex may change, suspend or withdraw any feature at any time without notice.

## 2. Fees
Fees are payable annually in advance and are non-refundable. Globex may increase fees on any
renewal by any amount on 30 days' notice.

## 3. Liability
Globex's liability is unlimited for any claim arising from Customer's use of the Software.
Customer's liability to Globex is limited to the fees paid in the prior month.

## 4. Intellectual property
All work product, configurations and improvements created by Customer while using the Software
are assigned to Globex on creation.

## 5. Data
Globex may share Customer data with its affiliates and partners for any purpose, including
product development and marketing.

## 6. Termination
Globex may terminate at any time on 7 days' notice. Customer may terminate only for Globex's
uncured material breach and forfeits prepaid fees if it does.

## 7. Renewal
This Agreement renews automatically for successive 36-month terms unless Customer gives written
notice at least 180 days before the end of the then-current term.

## 8. Non-compete
During the term and for 24 months afterwards, Customer will not use or develop any software that
competes with the Software.

This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware.
